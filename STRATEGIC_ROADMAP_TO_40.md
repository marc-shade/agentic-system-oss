**Early (Month 3)**:
- Pros: Faster optimization, more data
- Cons: Less baseline data, might bias too early

**Late (Month 6)**:
- Pros: More baseline data, better patterns
- Cons: Slower optimization, delayed benefits

**Build Language Understanding**:

- Cons: 2-3 years, massive resources
- Verdict: Not competitive

- Pros: Immediate capabilities, recursive improvement
- Cons: Dependent on external models
- Verdict: **Recommended strategy**

## Risk Mitigation

### Risk 1: Recursive Instability

**Scenario**: System makes changes that degrade own capabilities
**Mitigation**:
- Git rollback always enabled
- Confidence threshold remains high (70%)
- Meta-learning tracks degradation patterns
- Emergency stop mechanism

### Risk 2: Recursive Plateau

**Scenario**: Diminishing returns, improvements stop
**Mitigation**:
- Meta-learning finds new optimization strategies
- Domain expansion provides new opportunities
- Multi-model orchestration adds capabilities
- Research integration brings new ideas

### Risk 3: Safety Failure

**Scenario**: System bypasses safety mechanisms
**Mitigation**:
- Multi-layer safety (sandbox, rollback, confidence)
- Safety systems not improvable until proven
- Human review for critical changes
- Emergency kill switch

### Risk 4: Goal Misalignment

**Scenario**: System optimizes for wrong objectives
**Mitigation**:
- Clear performance metrics
- Objective evaluation system
- Regular human oversight
- Defined improvement goals
