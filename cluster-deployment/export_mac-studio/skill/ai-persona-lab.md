# AI Persona Lab

Create and manage AI personas for market research, user testing, and experimental studies using ai-persona-lab MCP.

## Persona Creation
**Individual Personas**
- Create custom personas with demographics (name, age, occupation)
- Automatic unique international macOS voice assignment
- Personality trait generation based on occupation/role
- Voice synthesis with natural speech patterns
- Claude Code subagent integration

**Voice Selection**
Premium international voices automatically assigned:
- US: Samantha, Alex, Victoria, Kate
- UK: Daniel, Serena, Oliver
- AU: Karen, Lee, Catherine
- Other: Amelie (FR), Anna (DE), Xander (NL), Diego (ES)

## Market Research Experiments
**Research Studies**
- Run market research with AI persona panels
- Target audience simulation and feedback
- Product validation and concept testing
- User journey mapping and UX research
- Competitive analysis from user perspectives

**Experiment Types**
- focus-group: Moderated group discussions
- survey: Structured questionnaire responses
- interview: One-on-one deep dives
- usability: Product/feature testing
- competitive: Competitor analysis

**Configuration**
- Custom participant counts (3-12 personas)
- Domain-specific expertise (tech, healthcare, finance, etc.)
- Demographic targeting (age, location, occupation)
- Multi-round studies with iteration

## Voice Management
**List Assigned Voices**
- View all persona-voice mappings
- Check voice availability and assignments
- Verify unique voice distribution
- Manage voice pool and assignments
