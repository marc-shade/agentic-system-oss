# Business Strategy & Validation

Comprehensive business strategy workflows: opportunity discovery, market validation, risk assessment, and compliance reporting.

## Gold Mining Framework
**Business Idea Discovery** (45-minute complete framework)

Six-phase agent orchestration for validated business opportunities:

**Phase 1: Market Discovery** (5-10 min)
- Market Explorer agent finds profitable niches (health, wealth, relationships)
- Sub-niche expansion and buying power analysis
- Focus verticals with proven demand

**Phase 2: Demand Validation** (10 min)
- Demand Validator analyzes search volume and trends
- Google Trends for market stability/growth
- High-intent query identification
- Commercial viability assessment

**Phase 3: Pain Point Mining** (10 min)
- Pain Point Miner extracts Reddit customer frustrations
- Authentic pain points with direct quotes
- Problem severity categorization
- Emotional trigger identification

**Phase 4: Gap Analysis** (10 min)
- Gap Analyzer identifies market opportunities
- Competitive landscape assessment
- Underserved segment discovery
- Solution space mapping

**Phase 5: Solution Design** (10 min)
- Solution Architect creates innovative approaches
- Value proposition development
- Business model ideation
- Go-to-market strategy

**Phase 6: Landing Page Creation** (5 min)
- Landing Page Builder creates conversion-optimized pages
- Value proposition messaging
- Social proof integration
- Call-to-action optimization

## Risk & Compliance Assessment

**Comprehensive Risk Reports** with structured analysis:

**Risk Assessment Tiers**
- Critical: Immediate attention required
- High: Address before deployment
- Medium: Monitor and mitigate
- Low: Acceptable with documentation

**Security Evaluation**
- Vulnerability scanning and analysis
- Security best practices compliance
- Data protection considerations
- Authentication/authorization review
- Threat modeling and mitigation

**Compliance Checklist**
- Industry standards (PCI DSS, HIPAA, SOC2, GDPR, FERPA)
- Internal policy compliance
- Quality gates status verification
- Documentation completeness audit
- Regulatory requirements mapping

**Approval Framework**
- Approved: Ready to proceed
- Approved with Monitoring: Conditional with oversight
- Conditional Approval: Must address specific items
- Not Approved: Critical blockers present

**Mitigation Strategies**
- Risk-by-risk resolution plans
- Timeline for risk remediation
- Resource requirement analysis
- Contingency planning
- Stakeholder communication

**Output Formats**
- Executive summary (board-ready)
- Technical deep-dive (engineering teams)
- Compliance audit report (regulatory)
- Risk matrix visualization (dashboards)
