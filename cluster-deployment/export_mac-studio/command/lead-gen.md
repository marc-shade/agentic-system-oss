# Lead Generation

Generate business leads by location and keywords.

## Usage
Use `mcp__lead-generator__generate_leads` with keywords and location.

## Examples
"Find restaurants in San Francisco"
"Generate leads for 'digital marketing agencies' in New York"
"Find plumbers within 10 miles of Chicago"

## Options
- Filter by business type
- Only businesses with websites
- Radius search (in meters)
- Maximum results limit

## Returns
- Business name, address, phone
- Website, ratings, hours
