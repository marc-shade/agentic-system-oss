[The paper discusses the EMPOWER algorithm from UC Berkeley and Princeton, which trains GPT coding assistants to:]

1. Complete predictable text segments with lower entropy
2. Stop precisely at points where creative input (high entropy token) is required
3. Hand control back to humans for meaningful decisions
4. Handle boring syntax and structure automatically
5. Recognize when unique human insight is needed

The key innovation is that the AI learns to assess the usefulness of its actions by estimating whether they enable a human to solve tasks more quickly, without debugging. The system uses entropy calculations to determine when to stop and let humans make creative decisions.

The authors reference a 2005 paper on "empowerment" from the University of Hertfordshire, which defined it as "an information theoretic capacity of an agent's actuation channel" - essentially about sharing information and enabling meaningful choices.
